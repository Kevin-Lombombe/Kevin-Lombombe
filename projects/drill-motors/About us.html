History

Drill Motors was started in 2010 by Ronald Burger as a small car dealership in Johannesburg, South Africa. From the beginning, the company aimed to offer high-quality cars and excellent service. Over time, Drill Motors has grown from a small business into one of the most trusted car dealerships in South Africa. The company originally focused on selling both new and used cars, quickly becoming known for its wide selection and knowledgeable staff. By always putting the customer first, Drill Motors has built strong, lasting relationships with its clients. This dedication to great service has been key to the company’s success, helping it to grow and improve to meet customers' needs. Today, Drill Motors is a leading car dealership in Johannesburg, recognized for its professionalism, quality service and commitment to growth. As the company continues to expand, it stays true to the values that have made it successful since the beginning.
